About Us

Building positive social impact across communities

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization established in 2020. The NGO works towards creating positive social impact through various welfare initiatives.

The organization focuses on education, women empowerment, environmental sustainability, animal welfare and youth skill development.

Through dedicated volunteers and impactful campaigns, InAmigos Foundation aims to uplift communities and inspire meaningful change.
